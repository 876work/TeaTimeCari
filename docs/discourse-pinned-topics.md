## Topic: Anonymous mode explained

Pinning recommendation: pin globally and keep visible until each member reads it.

```markdown
# Anonymous mode explained

Anonymous mode can help protect your public identity, but it does not make activity invisible to Tea Time Cari.

## The short version

- Other members won’t see your profile name, but admins may review abuse reports.
- Anonymous posts and comments must still follow the Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy.
- Authorized Tea Time Cari admins may review internal records for safety, moderation, legal compliance, abuse prevention, and enforcement.
- Anonymous mode must not be used for harassment, threats, false claims, impersonation, doxxing, leaked private content, or exposing someone’s private information.

## Before you post anonymously

Ask yourself:

1. Would I still post this if a moderator needed to review it?
2. Did I remove phone numbers, addresses, IDs, bank details, and private images?
3. Am I sharing to inform or ask for support, not to punish or embarrass someone?
4. Can I explain or support what I am saying if there is an abuse report?

Anonymous mode is for safer participation, not rule-breaking. If anonymous features are abused, Tea Time Cari may remove content, limit access, suspend an account, or take other enforcement action.
```

# Verification photo handling

Step 3 of registration captures a live selfie or government ID photo from the user's camera. This is sensitive identity material and must be handled as restricted data.

## User-facing disclosure

Before camera activation, registration must explain:

- why the photo is needed: account review, fraud prevention, and community safety;
- who can see it: authorized Tea Time Cari administrators involved in registration review or safety workflows;
- whether it appears publicly: never on profiles, posts, anonymous activity, or public community areas;
- how long it is kept: only as long as reasonably needed for registration review, fraud prevention, safety, legal, audit, dispute, or enforcement needs;
- how deletion works: users can contact support to request deletion where applicable, subject to legal and safety exceptions.

## Storage and access expectations

- Registration photos are currently submitted as base64 image data with the registration record.
- Admin review UI may display the image only to authenticated administrators with the appropriate review access.
- Do not copy, download, export, or share verification photos outside approved registration review, safety, fraud prevention, legal, audit, or dispute workflows.
- Verification photos are not product content and must not be reused for profiles, marketing, public posts, or community display.

## Deletion requests

When a user requests deletion of a verification photo, verify the request and remove the photo where applicable unless retention is needed for legal compliance, fraud prevention, safety, audit, dispute resolution, or enforcement of the Terms of Service.
